Clinical Explanation Evaluation

Study Instructions

The goal of this study is to understand how patient characteristics affect treatment efficacy. Specifically, this survey assesses explanations for how specific features modify treatment efficacy within randomized controlled trials (RCTs).

Based on your clinical expertise, you will be assigned one RCT in a specific specialty. For that trial, you will evaluate five explanations describing patient subgroup characteristics that may lead to different treatment effects.

Each explanation is rated using four qualitative criteria:

  • Logical Coherence
  • Causal Plausibility
  • Clinical Actionability
  • Evidence Quality

You will provide agree/disagree ratings for each criterion, with an optional brief summary in your own words. Your feedback is valuable and helps improve explanation quality to better support our development for clinical decision-making tools.

This study (IRB-ID: STUDY00025259) is IRB exempt.

Estimated time: ~5 minutes.
