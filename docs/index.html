Clinical Explanation Evaluation

Study Instructions

This study focuses on validating explanations derived from data-driven subgroup analyses across randomized controlled trials (RCTs).

Based on your clinical expertise, you will be assigned one RCT in a specific specialty. For that trial, you will evaluate five explanations describing patient subgroup characteristics that may lead to different treatment effects.

Each explanation is rated using four qualitative criteria:

  • Logical Coherence
  • Clinical Plausibility
  • Clinical Actionability
  • Evidence Quality

You will provide agree/disagree ratings for each criterion, with an optional brief summary in your own words. Your feedback helps improve explanation quality and reliability to better support clinical decision-making.

This study (IRB-ID: STUDY00025259) is IRB exempt. Estimated time: 5 minutes.
