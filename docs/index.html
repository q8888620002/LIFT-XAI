Clinical Explanation Evaluation

Study Instructions

The purpose of this study is to understand how patient characteristics may influence treatment effects in randomized controlled trials (RCTs).

You will be assigned to one RCT based on your selected clinical area of expertise. For that trial, you will review one or more sets of explanations (up to five explanations per set) describing patient subgroup characteristics that may be associated with different treatment effects. Please rate and submit each set separately.

For each explanation, you will evaluate five criteria:

  • Logical Coherence — Does the explanation make sense and follow a clear line of reasoning?
  • Causal Plausibility — Is the proposed relationship between the patient characteristic and treatment effect clinically or biologically plausible?
  • Clinical Actionability — Could the explanation help inform clinical interpretation or decision-making?
  • Evidence Quality — Is the explanation well supported by the information provided and/or relevant clinical evidence?
  • Novelty — Does the explanation identify an underexplored mechanism or subgroup not already well covered in existing guidelines or reviews?

For each criterion, you will indicate whether you agree or disagree. You may also provide an optional brief comment in your own words.

Your feedback will help us improve the quality of these explanations and support the development of clinical decision-making tools.

This study (IRB-ID: STUDY00025259) is IRB exempt.

Estimated time: ~5 minutes per set.
